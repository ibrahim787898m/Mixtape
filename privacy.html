Mixtape

Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Mixtape is a personal app that shows the songs from one person's own Spotify and YouTube Music playlists in one place. It is not a commercial service, and it is not offered to other people.

What it reads

  • Spotify: your playlists, the songs in the playlists you own or collaborate on, and your liked songs. It also uses your account to play music in the app.
  • YouTube: your playlists, their videos, and your liked videos (only music is kept). It may add songs to your playlists when you ask it to. It never deletes anything.

Where it's stored

The song list and the sign-in tokens are kept in a private Cloudflare Worker and database that belong to the app's owner. They are reachable only with the owner's private key. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics. This website itself stores nothing about you except settings in your own browser.

Removing access

Disconnect each service in Mixtape → Settings. You can also remove access at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for Spotify at spotify.com/account/apps.

Use of Google data

Mixtape's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
